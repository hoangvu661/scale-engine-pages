User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

According to Facebook Platform rules, users have the right to request deletion of their data associated with MCP Marketing Suite. If you wish to delete your data or revoke app authorization, please follow the simple steps below:

Option 1: Automatic Revocation & Deletion via Facebook

  1. Log in to your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate MCP in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove. This immediately revokes all access permissions and prompts Facebook to delete associated user credentials.

Option 2: Direct Deletion Request to Support

You can request complete deletion of any stored metadata or integration settings by sending an email with the subject "Data Deletion Request - MCP" to:

Email: vungochoang166@gmail.com

Please include your Facebook User ID or Ad Account ID. Our technical support team will process and permanently purge all associated integration records within 48 business hours.
