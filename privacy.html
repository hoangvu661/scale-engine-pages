Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MCP Marketing Suite ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect and manage Facebook Marketing and Advertising integrations.

1. Information We Access

Our application interacts with Meta Platforms, Inc. via the official Meta Graph API. We only access data strictly necessary to facilitate advertising management, including:

  • Basic Facebook profile information (User ID, name) for authentication purposes.
  • Advertising account identifiers, campaign metadata, ad sets, and ad creatives.
  • Performance analytics and metrics associated with ad campaigns.

2. How Information is Used

We use the data solely for:

  • Creating, configuring, and updating marketing campaigns as directed by the account owner.
  • Monitoring ad performance and generating optimization insights.
  • Ensuring technical integration stability and compliance with Meta policies.

3. Data Storage & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or advertising data with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and encrypted in your local runtime environment.

4. Data Deletion

You can request data deletion at any time by following our Data Deletion Instructions or by removing the app permissions from your Facebook Account Settings under Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites.
